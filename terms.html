1. What the app is

IRL Lore Pipeline ("the app") is a private, internal production tool. The owner of the IRL Lore channels operates it to publish the channels' own videos to YouTube (@IRLLoreTV) and TikTok. On YouTube it uploads videos, subtitles, thumbnails, and descriptions. On TikTok it sends videos to the account's drafts, and the owner publishes them in the TikTok app. The app is not sold, distributed, or offered to anyone else.

3. Authorized use

  • Only the channel owner signs in, through Google OAuth or TikTok Login Kit. The app acts only on the accounts that granted access.

4. Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time. For Google / YouTube, use security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. For TikTok, go to Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions in the TikTok app.
