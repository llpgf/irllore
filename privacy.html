Privacy Policy — IRL Lore Pipeline

Last updated: September 26, 2026

IRL Lore Pipeline ("the app") is a private production tool used only by the operator of the IRL Lore YouTube channel (@IRLLoreTV) to publish that channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses

  • With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app uploads videos, thumbnails, and subtitle files to the owner's own channel. It also sets their titles, descriptions, and translations.
  • It reads basic information about the owner's own channel and videos (for example video IDs and upload status) to confirm that uploads succeeded.
  • The app does not collect, read, or store data about viewers or any other YouTube users.

How data is stored and shared

  • The OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer and are never shared.
  • Video IDs and upload status are kept in a local production log.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • The app uses no cookies and no analytics.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Once access is revoked, the stored tokens stop working and are deleted from the operator's computer. To have any other stored data deleted, contact us at the address below. We will delete it within 30 days.
