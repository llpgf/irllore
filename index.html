IRL Lore

The real stories behind game characters · 遊戲角色背後的真實原型

IRL Lore is a fan-made video channel. Many game characters are based on real horses, real people, and real warships. We dig up the real history behind them and check every fact against primary sources.

Series 1: Uma Musume → the real racehorses. Videos are in English, with a Traditional Chinese audio track.

IRL Lore Pipeline is the channel's private production tool. It uses the YouTube Data API to upload our own videos, subtitles, and thumbnails to our own channel. It is not offered to other users.
